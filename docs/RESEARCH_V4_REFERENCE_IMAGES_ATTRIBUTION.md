# Research v4 reference drawings (display only)

The ten PNGs named `app/src/main/assets/research_v4_ref_<sign>.png`
are copies of TWKM standardised reference glyph drawings by Christian Prager
(University of Bonn), licensed CC BY 4.0 in the project's cited source.
Source dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/tadad/twkm-maya-glyphs
Credit: *Text Database and Dictionary of Classic Mayan (TWKM), University of
Bonn; standardised graph drawings by Christian Prager*. Licence: CC BY 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

These copies are **not used by the recogniser to look up an image, as
training examples, or to make a prediction**. The actual pretrained
Research v4 ONNX neural network runs on the user's captured or selected
image as before. They are displayed solely as a possible-match illustration
after inference, and the caption explicitly says this is not verification.

The original held-out image data has been exposed in previous research
reports. Displaying the reference in the app does not turn a screenshot
of it into an independent blind evaluation.

# Glyph activity journal

My Activity is now a glyph-focused explorer journal. The old text/image/voice totals and translation donut have been removed from this screen.

- Glyph scan totals come from retained, explicitly marked glyph history with valid, nonfuture timestamps; old text, voice and photo-OCR records are excluded.
- The seven-day scan chart uses local calendar boundaries, including daylight-saving changes. Labels and counts are exposed to screen readers. The rightmost column is today. Active days count scan days, not app openings or learning sessions.
- Glyph favourites link to the saved collection.
- Lesson completion and best quiz score read the same per-account device preferences as Learn. This is not cloud-synchronised progress.
- Milestones describe first scan, scanning on three days within the current seven-day window, a scan with multiple detected regions, and a glyph favourite. They reflect current data and are not permanent awards or proficiency claims.
- Recent explorations show up to three scan previews, suggested readings, timestamps and region counts. A filter switches between the last seven days and all retained history. Empty states lead to the scanner; cards and the History button open scan history. Continue learning and New exploration provide direct routes.

Deleting history changes the journal counts. No recognition scores, certainty thresholds or model weights were changed.

Validation includes date boundaries, future/invalid timestamps, exclusion of legacy records, DST, empty history, and an Android workflow for learning progress, recent filters, empty states and all navigation callbacks. Light and dark screenshots are captured by the runtime check.
